Legal & Transparency

Genesis Ocean is an independent, open-source visual explorer for the Genesis L1 ecosystem, created and maintained by Helyx.

Independence

Genesis Ocean is an independent community project, not an official Genesis L1 or GL1X product. Third-party names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Data accuracy and limitations

The explorer uses publicly available blockchain data. Information may be incomplete, delayed, unavailable, or inconsistent across data providers. Visual representations are intended to help visitors explore the network and should not be treated as authoritative blockchain records.

Genesis Ocean is provided for informational and educational purposes. It does not provide financial or investment advice.

Availability and external dependencies

The application depends on Genesis L1 public REST, RPC and WebSocket endpoints, the GL1X API, and external infrastructure. These services may experience interruptions or changes outside the control of Helyx.

Privacy and public blockchain data

Genesis Ocean is a read-only application. It does not require wallet connections, private keys, recovery phrases, or transaction signatures.

The application requests public blockchain information from external providers and loads fonts through Google Fonts. Hosting and external service providers may receive IP addresses, request metadata, and queried public blockchain identifiers under their respective privacy practices.

Blockchain addresses, transactions, validator information, and delegation records may be publicly accessible.
